This is an interesting idea, that a [a written work is an abstraction.](A%20written%20work%20is%20an%20abstraction.html). Because we are to [integrate instead of merely collecting](integrate%20instead%20of%20merely%20collecting.html), the process of refining ideas is the process of [creating new knowledge](Structure%20notes%20create%20new%20knowledge,%20rather%20than%20merely%20organizing%20it.html). The thought joins others [to become](Abstractions%20AKA%20layered%20complexity.html) a line of reasoning, lines of reasoning join to become discussions, discussions to sections, and sections to works. ([Ahrens](How%20to%20Take%20Smart%20Notes.html) 20-21)
