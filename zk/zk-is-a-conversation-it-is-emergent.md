I'm creating a *structure* for my [thoughts](a-zettel-is-a-thought.html), by *using* my thoughts. Wow.

as [meta-ideas](Indices.html) emerge ("thought a" and "thought b" eventually become "because a and b . . . "). The web of knowledge will grow and mature as I engage with it..

This is why I shouldn't [worry about the impedance of linking when capturing](No%20islands;%20link%20when%20capturing.html). That process of creating and reshaping my collection *is the entire purpose.*

It is the [creation-and-discovery-of-purpose](creation-and-discovery-of-purpose.html).
