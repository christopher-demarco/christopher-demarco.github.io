[Progressive disclosure](Progressive%20disclosure.html) is a useful virtue because it's harmonious with composability:
- if you only expose certain degrees of information, then you ought to chunk or segment it
- that leads naturally to atomicity
- which means it's much easier to assemble those atoms in novel ways!

See [skills-should-be-thin](skills-should-be-thin.html) and [ZK is excellent for writing because collections assemble upward into finished product](ZK%20is%20excellent%20for%20writing%20because%20collections%20assemble%20upward%20into%20finished%20product.html).
